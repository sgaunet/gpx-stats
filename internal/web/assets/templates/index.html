GPX Statistics

Upload a GPX file — or drop it on the map — to see your activity statistics and charts.

Privacy: your file is processed in memory and is not stored. Nothing is written to disk, and your file and its statistics never leave this server.
Map backgrounds are the one exception: they are loaded by your browser directly from the map provider, which can therefore see the area of the map you are looking at. Your GPX file is never sent to them.
